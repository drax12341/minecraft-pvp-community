The LEFT third should be very dark quiet navy almost black negative space blending naturally into the arena, for readable website heading overlay. Grounded believable Minecraft voxel scale, polished high-end game key art, no people, no characters, no lettering, no words, no logos, no UI, no watermarks. Dark blue-black overall palette, not neon rainbow, no bright sky. Crisp architecture, tasteful small particles. Output a landscape panoramic illustration.
